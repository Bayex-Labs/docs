# What is Bayex?

**Bayex** is a decentralized protocol built on top of the **Conditional Tokens (CT)** framework and **Uniswap V4**. It enables the creation, trading, and resolution of prediction markets and other contingent asset classes with unparalleled efficiency and flexibility.
